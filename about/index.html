About A1

Trucks for people who work

A1 Truck & Auto Sales is a used commercial truck dealer in Modesto, California, selling work trucks to contractors, landscapers, haulers and small fleets.

What we do

The right truck for the job

A work truck is a tool. It needs the right body, the right GVWR and a price that makes sense for the jobs it'll do. That's what we sell: used commercial trucks, listed with the specs that matter and a straight price.

You'll find us at 1400 N 9th St #10, just off N 9th St. Call ahead and we'll have the truck you're interested in pulled up and ready to look at.
